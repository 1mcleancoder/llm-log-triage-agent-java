# Payments troubleshooting overview

Use this page when checkout is failing and you do not yet know why.

1. Check whether failures are limited to one payment method or region.
2. Look for error codes in payments logs and open the matching runbook (ERR-40xx pages).
3. Compare traffic volume with the previous day. Sudden spikes often point to capacity problems.
4. Check recent deployments to payments and gateway.
5. If customers are affected for more than 10 minutes, open an incident and page the payments on-call.

This page gives general guidance only. Specific fixes live in the per-error runbooks.
